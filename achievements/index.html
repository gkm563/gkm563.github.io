04 Verification

Read it. Then check it.

An achievement page is only as good as its evidence trail. Here is how every record on this page can be checked.

01

Open the proof

Each card footer names its source. Six honours link to LinkedIn treasury overlays, one to Oracle CertView, one to TruScholar, and the Wikimedia work links to Gerrit and the OKI impact report.

02

Match the record

Title, issuer and date on the card match the award itself. Where the recognition was presented on stage, the photograph — not a badge — is the evidence.

03

Cross-check context

Roles and employment dates live on the roles ledger; certificates live on certifications. Nothing here needs to be taken on trust.

Looking for a specific record? Legacy honour IDs resolve to the matching card on this page. Start at the archive or open the full verification trail.

05 FAQ

Straight answers.

What this page covers, how to verify it, and why the academic record appears in two forms.

What exactly is on this page?
35 dated honours and recognitions from 2021 to 2026, sorted newest first and grouped into eight categories: academic, government, media, fellowship, open source, leadership, hackathon and credential. Roles, responsibilities and employment dates live on the separate roles ledger.
How do I verify a record?
Open the proof link in the footer of any card. Public evidence is linked where it exists: LinkedIn honour treasury overlays, Oracle CertView, TruScholar, Gerrit, the PrayagrajRooms platform, and case-study dossiers. Where the recognition was presented on stage, the photograph is the evidence.
Why do academic results appear twice?
The cards record the moment of recognition — a medal, a felicitation, a rank. The academic registry distils the same period into one clean year-by-year table: board percentages, SGPA and university ranks without the ceremony.
What is the strongest technical proof?
Fifteen-plus merged Wikimedia patches across MediaWiki Core, MinervaNeue, GrowthExperiments and Pywikibot; Best Contributor at Road to Wiki (IIIT Hyderabad); first place in the Wiki Contribution Marathon; and 65/70 in the AKTU Cyber Security theory examination.
Are media features included?
Yes. DD Kisan (Doordarshan Lucknow) filmed a three-day national documentary on the Vindhya Millets platform, and Dainik Jagran felicitated the Class 12 district rank in 2023.
