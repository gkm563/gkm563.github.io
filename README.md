## 🔍 Executive Profile: Who is Gautam Kumar Maurya (`gkm563`)?

**Gautam Kumar Maurya** (known across the global technology ecosystem as [**`gkm563`**](https://github.com/gkm563) or **GKM**) is an Indian **Full-Stack AI Engineer, Cyber Security Researcher, Data Scientist, and Open Source Developer** based in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh.

🎓 **Consistent Academic Topper (UIT / UGI / AKTU):** Gautam holds an exemplary academic record. In his B.Tech CSE (Data Science) program at **United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj (United Group of Institutions)**, he achieved **1st Rank across the CSE (Data Science) branch (SGPA 8.5)** and **5th Rank across the entire college** in AKTU examinations, felicitated by *Dainik Jagran* & the UP Board Secretary.

⚡ **Full-Stack AI & Secure Web Architect:** A sharp, agile technology innovator, Gautam engineers production-grade web systems, custom AI agent workflows, and scalable REST APIs with robust security standards.

🚀 **Founder Acceleration & Community Leadership:** Serving as **Vice President of GeeksforGeeks (GFG) Student Chapter UIT**, **Co-Lead of HackerRank UIT**, **Campus Lead of Open Source Connect India (OSCI'26)**, and **Head Lead Organizer for Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026)**, Gautam actively mentors 500+ student developers while co-founding real-world startups like [**PrayagrajRooms**](https://prayagrajrooms.in).
