About Me

Engineered for Performance, Driven by Innovation.

I am a Computer Science Engineer specializing in Data Science, Full-Stack Architecture, and Cyber Security. Currently pursuing B.Tech at United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj (AKTU), where I secured 1st Rank in CSE (DS) and Rank 5 College-wide in AKTU Examinations.

I have also represented India in international and state-level government programs: as a GIIP-2026 Scholar at Asian Institute of Technology (AIT Bangkok, Thailand) and as a certified cyber security intern under Amroha Police & UP Police (APCSIP-2026).
