01 About

Engineer by training. Builder by default.

A short account of the work, the systems behind it, and the standard I hold it to.

I build software where AI meets the real world — then I stay to ship it, measure it, and make it faster.

My work sits in three places: production products, open-source infrastructure, and the communities that make both possible. At Vindhya Millets I lead the digital product and the engineering behind a custom PHP/MySQL commerce platform with Razorpay checkout, webhook verification, hardened SQL and technical SEO. At PrayagrajRooms I co-founded the company and serve as CTO for a verified student-housing platform that crossed 5,000+ clicks in its launch week.

On the open-source side I contribute upstream to Wikimedia — MediaWiki core, MinervaNeue, GrowthExperiments, UploadWizard and Pywikibot — and serve as WikiClub Tech Envoy at UIT. I also train in cybersecurity with C3iHub IIT Kanpur and the UP Police cyber cell, which shapes how I think about data, trust and failure modes.

The common thread is systems that real people depend on. That means clear architecture, honest documentation, measurable performance, and design that respects the user's time.

“Build for people. Scale for impact.”
