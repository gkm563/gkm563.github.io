Frequently Asked Questions about Gautam's Events

Answers regarding Smart India Hackathon (SIH) leadership, UIT Coding Club (UCC), mega auditorium conferences, and student footprint.

Gautam single-handedly convenored and organized the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Internal Presentation Conclave at United Institute of Technology (UIT Prayagraj) — widely recognized as the most successful SIH conclave in the institution's history. He managed all operations: mobilizing 77 competing teams (totaling 462 students: 285 male / 177 female), vetting 37 problem statements across Software, Hardware & Open categories, nominating 50 teams (45 shortlisted for official MoE portal submission + 5 on merit waitlist), and directing 8 evaluation jury panels alongside 8 student volunteers.
Gautam is the Founding Member and President of the UIT Coding Club (UCC), established alongside esteemed faculty mentor Dr. Dhananjay Sharma. As President, Gautam designed and executed the UCC 2nd & 3rd Year Algorithmic Coding Challenge for 320+ competitive coders, authoring 6 novel problem statements, automated test harnesses, and hosting live leaderboard scoreboards.
Gautam co-directed the flagship Gateway to GATE conference bringing the Vice President of GeeksforGeeks Noida HQ (ex-ISRO scientist with AIR 12 and AIR 32 in GATE) to address 600+ students in the main auditorium. He also organized the Syntax Clash hackathon distributing official GFG backpacks, metal pens, stickers, and merit awards to 450+ coders, alongside a 3-Stage Coding Sprint series, annual chapter orientations, and intensive offline lab clinics.
As Co-Lead of the Gemini Builders Community under Google Student Ambassadors, Gautam conducted a campus-wide auditorium masterclass for 600+ students on 'Vibe Coding' — demonstrating prompt engineering, agentic software synthesis, and deploying full-stack web applications live on stage at the speed of thought.
Gautam has mobilized over 4,200+ cumulative attendees across 17+ technical events, summits, hackathons, and algorithm leagues, featuring 4 mega 500+ student auditorium summits, 500+ distributed swags and bags, and institutional honors from College Principals and Deans.
